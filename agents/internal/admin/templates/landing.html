For founders who cannot staff an office yet

The rest of the company, before you can pay for the rest of the company.

Most early startups can ship a product. They still need someone on the books, someone answering customers, someone hosting the community, someone who remembers the lead. Those seats are expensive. This project exists so a lean team — starting with us in Lagos — can keep that work moving without hiring a full back office.

Why this exists

Payroll is the wall. A bookkeeper, a community manager, a sales intern, and an ops person are four salaries a pre-revenue company cannot carry. The founder becomes all four, then the product waits. We are building a synthetic office for the non-technical work: receipts, polite replies, the group chat, the follow-up, the “are we eligible for this grant?” — the parts that keep a business alive while the builders stay on the product.

TheMobileProf is eating this first. If it holds, other startups in the same position should be able to run the same way: one number, one inbox, an office that answers, and a human only when judgment is required.

  • Front desk Customers get a reply tonight. You are not glued to WhatsApp.
  • Books Spend is recorded. You do not wait for a full-time accountant.
  • Pipeline Names, numbers, and next steps are kept. Leads do not live in your head.
  • Community The room stays warm. The founder is not always the host.
  • Ops The messy middle: vendors, flags, grants, the work between invoices.
  • Product sense Test the idea before you staff a research intern you cannot pay.
