Incubation bets under one CAC company. Stage is Idea / Testing / Selling / Fundable. The worker only proposes. You click Accept on Decide — that is your yes. A field person goes to the place and brings names back.

  1. Open Projects. The Run tab is the wizard — same base kit for every bet: Card, Face, Decide, Dates, Work, then this gate, then Legal / Files / SEO as needed.
  2. Ask placement writes a proposal. Accept / Amend / Reject on Decide changes the stage. The worker cannot commit.
  3. Field week: send a human for one mission (mortgage rates, mechanics, a PHC). They return named people and a next step. Notes without a yes do not count.
  4. Optional: /validate analyse_evidence with those names in the task. Still a proposal. You click Accept on Decide when you mean GO / PIVOT / KILL.

People on the roster are login. Seat a field person as assistant on the project for that week. Do not give them every worker unless they also handle AI threads.
