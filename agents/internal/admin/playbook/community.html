Room voice. Welcome, FAQ, moderation, announcements. Not sales and not the books desk.

  1. WhatsApp or Telegram group with no prefix → community.
  2. DM: /cm or /community plus the ask.
  3. Unauthorized WhatsApp /accounts, /ops, /legal, or /hr is rewritten to community with accounts_denied: true. Books, Legal, and HR never run.
  4. Manual smoke: Smoke: community HTTP (smkCommHttp0001).
