Room voice with a mandate per Academy Telegram group. Welcome, weekly intro, progress, lesson help, FAQ, moderation. Not sales and not the books desk. Cloud API WhatsApp cannot join ordinary groups.

  1. On Mandates, write the brief and the catalog (Academy LMS is seeded from the public course list). Leave a course off the catalog and the worker must escalate instead of guessing.
  2. Create the Academy Telegram bot (BotFather). /setprivacy Disable. Add it to the student group. Send a message. Copy context_data.telegram_chat_id from the Academy Telegram n8n execution onto the mandate.
  3. Unprefixed group chat uses that mandate. /cm or /community also works. Ops prefixes (/accounts, /approve, …) are refused on this bot.
  4. Monday 09:00 Lagos, Community weekly intro (commWeeklyPing01) posts in each room that has a Telegram chat id (and any leftover WhatsApp group id).
  5. Do not add the ops Telegram bot to the student group. Unauthorized WhatsApp /accounts, /ops, /legal, or /hr is still rewritten to community with accounts_denied: true.
  6. Manual smoke: Smoke: community HTTP (smkCommHttp0001).

Specimen 01 · welcome a group

Someone joins the Lagos founders WhatsApp group.

{
  "task_description": "Welcome a new member who just joined the Lagos founders WhatsApp group and ask what they are building.",
  "context_data": {
    "channel": "whatsapp",
    "chat_kind": "group"
  }
}

task_type welcome. Short phone-friendly reply. No markdown tables. escalate_to_founder false unless they need a human.

Specimen 02 · access denied

A WhatsApp number that is not on People → whatsapp_accounts sends /accounts I paid MTN 15000.

{
  "task_description": "Write a WhatsApp DM to this person in second person. They are not authorized for the company books, legal, or HR desk.",
  "context_data": {
    "channel": "whatsapp",
    "chat_kind": "dm",
    "accounts_denied": true,
    "department": "community"
  }
}

Fixed Go reply (no LLM): they cannot book expenses, draft NDAs, or file applicants from that number. Suggest /growth or /cm. Amounts, vendors, contract text, and CVs are not repeated. n8n does not call Books write, Legal, or HR.

Specimen 03 · FAQ

Group: /cm When do we meet this month?

task_type faq. If the worker does not know the date, it says so and sets escalate_to_founder true. Telegram/WhatsApp just send output_text — there is no separate founder ping yet. Escalate still files a Community job on the Community desk.

Not wired · founder ping on escalate

When you wire a Telegram card for escalate_to_founder, add the specimen: which chat id, what the card contains, and that community still replies in the original thread. Until then the Community desk job is the ping.

Not wired · LMS lesson token

Course titles are public (GET https://api.themobileprof.com/api/courses). Lesson video and quizzes need a Bearer token from POST /api/auth/login. Prefer a dedicated community-bot LMS user, secret in the VM .env — not a founder password in git. Until then the catalog is titles and syllabus lines only.
