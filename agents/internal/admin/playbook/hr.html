  1. Desk HR: Ask JD (intern / contractor / assistant / employee). Accept opens the role.
  2. info@ that looks like a CV or “I am applying” goes to HR, not growth. n8n files a proposed application. Customer mail stays growth.
  3. Telegram (or allowlisted WhatsApp): /hr plus the ask. Chat is flags; the desk is the filing path unless email already filed.
  4. Shortlist or Turn away on the desk. Reasons live on the row. Do not use info@ /approve.

People on People are login/access. Applicants are not that. WhatsApp /hr is allowlisted like /accounts.
