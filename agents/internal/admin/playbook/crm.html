Not wired · follow-up reminders

The worker can fill next_action_by. Nothing cron-pokes you on that date. When you wire a reminder (Telegram at 09:00 Africa/Lagos), add the specimen: where the date is stored, and that it must not double-text the customer.

Not wired · notification sequences

Email and WhatsApp outbound will fire from this person’s timeline, not from a third-party CRM. A human still Accepts or /approve. Do not auto-send. When you wire it, add the specimen: which event starts the sequence, and that it must not double-text.

Not wired · upsell plays

Won stays on the book. The offer field is what they already bought. An upsell play later proposes the next offer on that same person. It does not invent a second WhatsApp number or a second Books org.
