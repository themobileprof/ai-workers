Pipeline notes for a person or company. n8n upserts a Zoho Books contact on the existing Books OAuth. There is no Zoho CRM app. Inbound info@ is correspondence, not a subject line.

  1. WhatsApp or Telegram: /crm Ada from Acme in Yaba asked for a demo. Phone 0803…
  2. A customer mail to info@ calls CRM with the letter and the unsent draft. Notes on the Books contact hold the rolling story. /crm looks those notes up before it answers.
  3. Never invent email or phone. If WhatsApp context_data.from is digits, that is the phone.

Specimen 01 · qualify a lead

Telegram: /crm Chinedu from Apex Motors, Ikeja, wants the WhatsApp books assistant. WhatsApp 2348091112222.

{
  "task_description": "Chinedu from Apex Motors, Ikeja, wants the WhatsApp books assistant. WhatsApp 2348091112222.",
  "context_data": {
    "channel": "telegram",
    "department": "crm"
  }
}

task_type qualify_lead. record_lead true with contact_name, company_name, phone, pipeline_stage, intent_score, next_action. n8n POST/PUT Books /contacts. Reply includes a one-line audit that the contact was saved.

Specimen 02 · auto from high-intent growth

WhatsApp 1:1 (no /crm): someone names themselves and asks to start this week.

Every successful customer WhatsApp DM with a phone upserts a Zoho Books contact (Growth or CRM). Ops Telegram still upserts on /crm or high intent. Access-denied community replies do not upsert.

Specimen 03 · update a record

/crm Apex Motors is now at proposal. They want a 30-day pilot, still 2348091112222.

task_type update_record or pipeline. Same upsert. Do not invent revenue.

Specimen 04 · inbound email story

A client mails info@ about a 30-day pilot. Later: /crm follow up Apex.

task_type follow_up. context_data.correspondence is their letter; draft_reply is the unsent info@ draft; existing_contact.notes is the prior Books story. Notes stay a short rolling story, not Inbound email: subject. CRM does not send the mail.

Not wired · follow-up reminders

The worker can fill next_action_by. Nothing cron-pokes you on that date. When you wire a reminder (Telegram at 09:00 Africa/Lagos), add the specimen: where the date is stored, and that it must not double-text the customer.
