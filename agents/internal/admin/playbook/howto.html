This is the operator manual for the signed-in desk. Workers propose; you click Accept. Chat never Accepts, never Places an ad, and never sends mail or WhatsApp on its own. Seat sheets stay on How you fit. JSON specimens sit on each worker sheet. The same map in git is HOW_YOU_RUN.md.

  1. Sign in on the desk. Owner and BDM land on Board. Everyone else lands on the desks ticked on their People card.
  2. Ask on the desk, or send ops Telegram with a prefix, or send plain 1:1 text so the bot picks one desk. The worker proposes.
  3. Accept or Turn away on the desk. When you Turn away, you may type a short reason so the next draft on that bet can avoid the same mistake. /approve only sends a pending info@ draft. That command is not Accept.

Four doors

Desk

The website at /admin/ is where you Accept jobs, Place ads, edit Cards, and amend Defaults. Help? at the bottom right fills the same form as the page you are on; it never Accepts.

Ops Telegram

This is the staff bot. Prefixes follow the desks ticked on your People card. Plain 1:1 text lets the bot pick one desk and do that work in the chat. Customer WhatsApp cannot run those office prefixes.

Customer WhatsApp

A 1:1 chat is a sales conversation. When the message includes a phone number, the desk files that person on Office → CRM. This number is not a class group and cannot run Books, Legal, or Delivery.

Academy Telegram

Student rooms use the Community host only. Do not add the ops bot to class.

Lagos clocks

  1. At 06:00 and 06:10, n8n pulls organic GA4 and Search Console for the last 28 days onto Defaults → Traffic. Quote only those snapshots.
  2. At 06:20, paid GA4 for the last 7 days and the funnel land on the Growth book. If there were paid sessions, Go files one proposed Paid traffic glance job for that Lagos day. The glance does not Place.
  3. At 07:00, ads prune runs. After a campaign has run a full week, it pauses a cell that spent with 0 clicks and turns off wasted Lagos dayparts. Conversions wait two weeks, at least 5 clicks, and tracking that already fired.
  4. At 08:30, ops Telegram lists today’s Delivery tasks, Coming up dates, and (for owner and BDM) CRM due rows.
  5. At 09:30 the Academy host checks in. At 16:00 it sends ₦500 even when nobody earned it. Monday 09:00 is the intro, when the linked bet’s stage allows pings.

New campaigns start PAUSED unless you set ENABLED on Defaults → Ads. ClubKonnect keys stay in the VM .env.

Run a bet

  1. Open Projects, then the bet, then the Run tab. Every bet uses the same kit: Card, Face, Decide, Dates, then Work.
  2. The Card holds who pays, this week’s line, the public URL, the GitHub repo, and the Funnel line copied from TRAFFIC.md.
  3. Ask placement, then Accept on Decide to write the stage. Chat never stamps GO, PIVOT, or KILL.
  4. /validate can propose a hypothesis, mission, and evidence. Accept on Product writes those fields onto the Card. Accept on Decide is still what writes the stage.

Growth — sales, pitch, and ads

  1. A customer WhatsApp 1:1 is a sales conversation. When the message includes a phone number, the desk files that person on Office → CRM. On ops Telegram, /growth drafts social copy, walks a pitch deck, or drafts Search ads; it still cannot spend.
  2. For a pitch, open the project → Deck → Start session. Chat Guy Kawasaki’s ten slides from the Card. Accept on Growth, paste the markdown into Slides yourself, and /file the PDF under product.
  3. For ads, put a public URL on the Card first. Open Ads and walk the six steps (final URL, geo and keyword cells, keywords, headlines ≤30 characters, descriptions ≤90, then Pexels queries). Cells are named places plus themes already on the Card, not age or gender. Go drops a city that is not on the Card.
  4. Accept, then either follow Book it yourself in the Google Ads or Meta UI from the pack, or click Place on Google Search or Meta. Do not do both for the same channel. Chat never Places. Place is a cheap probe at the platform-minimum daily budget, never more than remaining funds divided by 90, exact match only, Google Search only. The campaign name and utm_campaign are the bet slug.
  5. To evaluate without spending, read the GA4 block on the Growth book, ask /growth How are the HomeGauge ads doing in GA4?, or open the morning glance job. Funnel events must already fire on the site (Card / TRAFFIC.md). Do not invent generate_lead.

n8n holds Google Ads and Facebook Ads OAuth. Account ids live on Defaults → Ads. Google sunset developer tokens on 9 September 2026; enable the Google Ads API on the Cloud project that owns the credential and apply for Explorer (or higher). Search the Pexels phrases yourself.

Community, CRM, Books, Legal, Delivery, and Files

  1. Community. Create one mandate per Academy Telegram room. Students DM the Academy bot the email on their LMS account. At 16:00 Lagos the host sends ₦500 even when nobody earned it. Campus weeks belong on Dates, not on Community.
  2. CRM. Office → CRM is the conversion book. WhatsApp, info@, and /crm file a timeline on the person. The clerk does not send. n8n upserts a Books contact when there is an email, or once the stage is qualified or later.
  3. Accounts. On ops Telegram send a receipt with /accounts. n8n posts the Zoho document. Go never computes VAT. Customer WhatsApp cannot book this.
  4. Legal. Ask a draft on the bet, Accept on Legal, and copy the text yourself. Watch flags labour, CAC, or NDPR after another desk answers. The clerk cannot email the other party.
  5. Delivery. This is the week board. The project’s stage caps how many tasks may be open. Fathom notes arrive as a proposed plan. The 08:30 ping lists tasks, Coming up, and CRM due.
  6. Files. On ops Telegram send /file or drop a PDF. A catalog match files itself. Odd paths wait for Accept. Office papers use the slug themobileprof.

What the clerk remembers

Staff Accept and Turn away write a labeled episode on the desk. An optional Turn-away reason is shown to the next draft on that desk and bet. Owner and BDM read those rows at Docs → Episodes. Academy Telegram and customer WhatsApp are not stored there. The model weights do not change, and Chat still never Accepts.

Defaults versus secrets

Ids (Zoho, Ads customer, GA4 property, Drive) live on Defaults. OAuth tokens, ClubKonnect keys, and the GA4 JSON key stay in n8n or the VM .env. Saving one tab does not wipe the others.
