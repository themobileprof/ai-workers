Classifier only. It names the Zoho document. Zoho Books owns VAT, WHT, and the ledger. WhatsApp /accounts is allowlisted on People.

  1. Allowlisted WhatsApp or any Telegram ops chat: /accounts I paid Jumia 18500 for office paper today.
  2. n8n reads structured_data.zoho_action and runs Books write (booksWriteDoc0001).
  3. You get the audit trail on the same chat (expense / bill / invoice id + Paystack link, or a lookup summary).
  4. Hypotheticals must say “do not record”. Smoke: Smoke: accounts HTTP — that sample is preview, must not post.

Actions: expense (already paid) · bill (we owe) · invoice (we raise; Paystack link if an email is present) · lookup (read Books) · preview (math in Books, post nothing).

Specimen 01 · paid receipt

/accounts Paid Shoprite 12500 NGN for office snacks on 13 Sep 2026

{
  "task_description": "Paid Shoprite 12500 NGN for office snacks on 13 Sep 2026",
  "context_data": {
    "channel": "telegram",
    "department": "accounts"
  }
}

zoho_action expense, record_expense true, currency NGN, vendor_name Shoprite. n8n POST /expenses with tax_id from Zoho settings. Reply cites the expense id. Go output has no VAT arithmetic.

Specimen 02 · we owe a vendor

/accounts Acme Consulting Ltd invoiced us 100000 NGN for software development, not yet paid

zoho_action bill. n8n upserts the vendor and POST unpaid bill. WHT is not a second expense — withhold on vendor payment in Books.

Specimen 03 · invoice we raise

/accounts Invoice Apex Motors 250000 NGN for a 30-day pilot, bill billing@apexmotors.ng

zoho_action invoice. n8n upserts the customer (with email), POSTs the invoice, marks it sent, then creates a Paystack Payment Request for Books total × 100 kobo. Chat audit includes https://paystack.com/pay/{request_code}. No email in the source → Books invoice only; never invent an address.

Specimen 04 · lookup / cash position

/accounts What do we still owe, and cash P&L?

zoho_action lookup. n8n GETs unpaid bills/invoices, recent expenses, cash P&L. Nothing is written.

Specimen 05 · hypothetical (must not post)

/accounts What is VAT and WHT on a 100000 NGN invoice from Acme Consulting Ltd? Do not record this.

{
  "task_description": "What is VAT and WHT on a 100000 NGN invoice from Acme Consulting Ltd for software development? Do not record this, it is a hypothetical.",
  "context_data": {}
}

zoho_action preview. n8n applies the tax percentage stored in Books and posts nothing. If this ever creates an expense, that is a bug.

Specimen 06 · they paid

Paystack dashboard webhook POST /webhook/paystack-paid. n8n verifies the transaction (does not trust the body), then POST /customerpayments on the Zoho invoice from Paystack metadata. Duplicate webhooks are no-ops once Books balance is 0. Lookup then shows the invoice paid.

WhatsApp image → OCR → accounts is not wired. When it is, add the specimen: media download, what goes in task_description vs context_data, and that a failed OCR must not invent amounts.
