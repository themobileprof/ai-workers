The owner ticked one or more workers on your People card. Those are the only workers you see. You land on them when you sign in — not on Board.

  1. Sign in. One worker → you are on it. Several → pick from Workers.
  2. Ask in a sentence a human would say — type it on this page, or walk it in Help?. Wait for a proposed job. Help? never Accepts.
  3. Open the job. Chat a correction if the draft is wrong. Chat does not Accept.
  4. Accept or Turn away. Specialized books (Legal, SEO, Growth decks, Delivery, Community mandates, Projects) sit one click from that worker if you were ticked for them.

Ask → Accept

  1. You “Draft an NDA for Ada on Mechazone.”
  2. Worker Files a proposed job. Nothing is accepted yet.
  3. You Chat “counterparty is Ada, not the shop.” Then click Accept.
  4. Book The NDA (or JD, or placement) moves. Telegram /approve did not do this — that command only sends a pending info@ email.

Customer WhatsApp

Bare 1:1 is a customer (growth). Anyone can message it. Books, legal, SEO, and delivery never run here. A customer who types /accounts gets a no.

Do

Open the worker page for the desk you run — that is the specimen book with examples. Community hosts: Community. Books: Accounts.

Do not

Do not expect chat to Accept. Do not reuse /approve for a contract. Do not add the ops bot to the Academy group. People is login and Delivery roster, not applicants.
