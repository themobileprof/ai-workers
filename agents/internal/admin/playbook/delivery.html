One PM across every bet. Academy is not special. The project’s stage (the coloured label) sets how many open tasks it may hold. Each person’s hours/week on People is the ceiling. Seat them on Face to onboard — that is not hiring.

  1. Owner adds a person on People and sets hours/week (slow default 6).
  2. Seat them on the bet’s Face tab. Owner and BDM can take any bet without a seat.
  3. Open Delivery. Add a task or Ask plan. The worker proposes; Accept on the Delivery desk writes the rows.
  4. Parked and killed bets get zero tasks and no morning ping. Idea = 1 open task. Testing = 3. Selling = 5. Fundable = 4.
  5. WhatsApp /today or a reply to the morning ping. /idea homegauge pins that bet. Unprefixed 1:1 stays growth. /approve is still only the info@ draft.
