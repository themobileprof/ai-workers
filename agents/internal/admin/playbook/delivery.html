One PM across every bet. Academy is not special. The project’s stage (the coloured label) sets how many open tasks it may hold. Each person’s hours/week on People is the ceiling. Seat them on Face to onboard — that is not hiring.

  1. Owner adds a person on People and sets hours/week (slow default 6).
  2. Seat them on the bet’s Face tab. Owner and BDM can take any bet without a seat.
  3. Open Delivery. Add a task or Ask plan. The worker proposes; Accept on the Delivery desk writes the rows.
  4. Parked and killed bets get zero tasks and no morning ping. Idea = 1 open task. Testing = 3. Selling = 5. Fundable = 4.
  5. Ops Telegram: write what happened in your own words, or reply to the morning ping the same way. You do not number tasks and you do not wait for the bot to name Delivery. /today still works as a prefix. /idea homegauge pins that bet. Customer WhatsApp cannot run Delivery. /approve is still only the info@ draft.
  6. Put the bet slug in the calendar title. After Fathom finishes, a proposed plan lands on Job chat. Accept writes the tasks. Ops Telegram /pm still pastes notes by hand.

Specimen 01 · ask a week

Delivery → Ask plan: “Turn this-week notes into tasks for me and the BDM.”

Worker names tasks per bet. Extra items sit on the backlog. save_plan true. The plan writes after a delay unless you Turn away. Accept still works now. Chat does not write the board.

Specimen 02 · Telegram field report

Reply to “Today on the desk”, or just write: We went to Ikorodu General Hospital yesterday, spoke with the ANC, left fliers, and the HOD said next Monday is the next slot. We spent N20,000 on fuel.

The clerk logs the narrative on Delivery, upserts the hospital (or HOD) on CRM without inventing email or phone, inserts the Monday date, appends a Card note, matches your open tasks by title, and files the fuel as a proposed Accounts job. It does not post Zoho from that mixed paragraph. Work for someone else stays a proposal. Chat never Accepts.

Specimen 03 · parked bet

Finchest is parked. Ask for Finchest tasks.

No slots. Worker says this stage allows no tasks. It does not invent work.

Specimen 04 · Fathom notes

Calendar title names the bet (for example academy weekly). Fathom records, then the webhook fires.

Delivery proposes tasks inside stage slots. save_plan true. You Accept on Job chat. If an invitee is already on Office → CRM (and not on People), the desk files a call event on that person — it does not create a contact and does not send. Extra commitments stay held in the reply.

Specimen 05 · SEO offsite listings

A human adds listings this company already has as ordinary Delivery tasks. Onsite SEO is Cursor in the bet repo.

Stage caps apply; extras sit on Backlog. Chat never Accepts. Nobody sends mail or WhatsApp from this office for listings.

Specimen 06 · campus training

The bet’s Dates tab already names the week. On that date, Add task on Delivery for the work, or add it here. Click Done when it is real.

The date lives on the bet. This book holds the work. The stage still limits how many tasks can be open.

Do not

Do not brief an Academy-only person about Finchest on WhatsApp. Name the bet (/idea academy) or sit on one card. Office may see every bet. Do not use customer WhatsApp to chase a meeting plan. Do not treat Fathom as Accept.
